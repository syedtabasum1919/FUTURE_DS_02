# FUTURE_DS_02 – Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## 📌 Task Overview

This project is part of the **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship Program**.

The objective of this task is to analyze customer churn data, identify the factors that influence customer retention, and generate useful insights that can help businesses reduce customer churn.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze customer churn patterns.
* Understand the factors associated with customer churn.
* Compare customer characteristics between churned and retained customers.
* Identify important customer segments with higher churn rates.
* Provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 📊 Dataset

**Dataset:** Telco Customer Churn Dataset

The dataset contains information about customers, including:

* Customer demographics
* Tenure
* Contract type
* Payment method
* Monthly charges
* Total charges
* Internet services
* Customer churn status

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Google Colab
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project includes analysis of:

* Overall customer churn rate
* Churn by contract type
* Churn by tenure
* Churn by monthly charges
* Churn by payment method
* Churn by internet service
* Customer demographic patterns
* Comparison of retained and churned customers

## 📈 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify customer groups that are more likely to churn and highlights the factors associated with customer retention.

Some important areas analyzed include:

* Contract type
* Customer tenure
* Monthly charges
* Payment methods
* Internet services

These insights can help businesses understand customer behavior and focus their retention strategies on higher-risk customer segments.

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can:

* Improve customer retention strategies for high-risk segments.
* Encourage customers to choose longer-term contracts.
* Provide targeted offers to customers with higher churn risk.
* Improve customer service and engagement.
* Monitor customers with short tenure and high monthly charges.
